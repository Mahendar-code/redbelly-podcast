> Then why are we pushed in the digital asset space of dealing with US dollars, right? And why? Because they've been there longer, and there is that dominance, and there is that comfort. And, you know, no doubt that digital asset space is priced in US dollars. So, you know, that plays quite an important role in that. However, it all then boils down to every time you're using a US dollar, that is, and especially a US dollar stablecoin, it's money that's not sitting onshore. It's money that's not backing your asset onshore. And so supporting the Australian ecosystem by using a blockchain like Redbelly that is an Australian-built and Australian-operated blockchain is pretty important.
