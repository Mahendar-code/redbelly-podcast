## What each check means

- **Audio matches stated source timestamp:** the first 10 seconds of each clip's audio are cross-correlated with the official episode around the timestamp given in `CLIPS.md`. An offset of zero proves the timestamp points at exactly this audio.
- **Subtitles = proof-read text:** the words in the SRT file are the proof-read text in `clips.json`, word for word.
- **Subtitles vs independent transcript:** each finished clip is transcribed again with Whisper `distil-large-v3`, a fourth model that was not used to make the subtitles, and compared with the subtitle text. The word error rate is measured after folding Australian and US spellings together (-ise and -ize endings), reading "+" as "plus", joining split compounds (guard rails, guardrails), and dropping fillers and stammered repeats, which clean verbatim subtitles leave out. The raw rate is shown beside it, and every remaining difference is listed below so it can be checked by ear.
- **Every spoken word inside a cue:** each timed word falls inside the time range of a subtitle cue.
- **Speaker in frame:** five frames per second of the finished clip are checked with face detection (YuNet) and face recognition (SFace) against reference frames of the named speaker.
- **Names, terms, credits, dashes:** subtitles, docs and the page are searched for misspellings of Redbelly terms and names, for personal credits or handles, and for en or em dashes and emoji, which the Redbelly DAO brand kit rules out.
