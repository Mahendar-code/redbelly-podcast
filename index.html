Behind the cuts

Everything here can be rebuilt from the official episode files with the scripts in pipeline/.

Newest episodes first

The official episode page, redbelly.network/ama, has Episode 29 (9 July 2026) as its newest Redbelly Insights episode. Episodes 25 to 29 were downloaded from the links on that page and read through, newest first.

A moment made the cut only if it was about Redbelly technology, Project Acacia or Tokeniser, landed one point that a newcomer can follow, and said something no other clip in the pack says.

No edits inside a clip

Each clip runs straight through from its first word to its last. Nothing is removed from the middle and nothing is stitched together, so a speaker cannot be made to say something they did not say.

In and out points sit in the silence between two words, and the audit checks each clip's audio against the episode at the listed time.

Subtitles checked three ways

Three speech models (Whisper small.en, medium.en and large-v3) each transcribed every clip. The subtitles were settled word by word against all three, and names and titles were checked on redbelly.network, tokeniser.com and each guest's company site.

Terms follow the brief: Redbelly, RBNT, Tokeniser, Project Acacia, in Australian spelling. Stammers and words like um are left out. The lines are burned in with the current word picked out in red, and also supplied as SRT.

The speaker stays on screen

Face recognition finds the speaker in each camera shot of the 16:9 episode, so the square window always sits on the person talking rather than the host. Within a shot the window does not move.

Where the episode itself shows the host for a moment while the guest talks, the guest's nearest frame is held and the sound carries on. SOURCES.md lists each of these.

One level for all six

Only the episode's own sound is used. Every clip is measured and brought to -14 LUFS, the level X, YouTube and Instagram play at, with a limiter holding true peaks below -2 dBTP, so the six play at the same volume.

Nothing has been added: no music, no effects, no outside footage.

In the DAO's colours

Clips and page use the Redbelly DAO Task Board brand kit: slate backgrounds, one red accent, Be Vietnam Pro for words, JetBrains Mono for numbers such as episode and time, and the DAO logo on dark with no plate.

Headline, name plate and captions sit above the strip at the bottom where X, Shorts, Reels and TikTok draw their own buttons.
