How the pack was made

Everything here can be rebuilt from the official episode files with the scripts in tools/.

Latest episodes first

The official episode page, redbelly.network/ama, lists Episode 29 (9 July 2026) as the most recent Redbelly Insights episode. Episodes 25 to 29 were downloaded from the official links on that page and transcribed, newest first.

Only passages about Redbelly technology, Project Acacia or Tokeniser were considered. Each clip had to make one point that holds up for someone who has never heard of Redbelly, and no two clips could make the same point.

One continuous passage per clip

Every clip is a single uninterrupted stretch of the episode. Nothing inside a clip is cut, reordered or joined, so no quote can be edited into a different meaning.

Clips start and end on sentence boundaries, and the exact source range is listed for each clip and checked against the episode audio.

Subtitles you can trust

The subtitle text was proof-read word for word against three separate Whisper transcriptions (small.en, medium.en and large-v3), and every name and role was checked against official pages: redbelly.network, tokeniser.com and the guest's own company site.

Redbelly terms follow the brief: Redbelly, RBNT, Tokeniser, Project Acacia, with Australian spelling throughout. Hesitations such as "um" are left out. Subtitles are burned in, with the spoken word highlighted, and also shipped as SRT.

Speaker in shot

The episodes are 16:9. Each camera shot is found automatically and the speaker is located by face recognition, so the vertical window is set on the person speaking, never on the host. The window is fixed for each shot, so it never drifts or wobbles.

Where the episode itself cuts to the host for a moment, the speaker's last frame is held while the audio carries on. Those moments are listed in CLIPS.md.

Even audio, nothing added

Only the episode's own audio is used. Each clip is normalised with two-pass EBU R128 loudness to -14 LUFS integrated, the level X, YouTube and Instagram play at, with a peak limiter that keeps the true peak below -2 dBTP. All six sound the same.

No music, sound effects or third party material has been added.

Brand and layout

Clips and page follow the Redbelly DAO Task Board brand kit: slate surfaces, one red accent, Be Vietnam Pro for copy, JetBrains Mono for machine values such as episode numbers and timestamps, and the DAO logo on dark without a plate.

The hook, the speaker's name and role and the captions all sit clear of the bottom band where X, Shorts, Reels and TikTok draw their own buttons and text.
